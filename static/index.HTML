13 — Model limitations

  • Simulated agents are a simplified statistical representation, not real people — individual predictions are meaningless, only the aggregate distribution is informative.
  • Outputs depend entirely on the assumptions and parameter values entered above; changing "Model assumption" values changes the results accordingly.
  • Real human behaviour cannot be predicted with certainty — actual adoption and compliance may diverge from this projection in either direction.
  • Real-world implementation involves logistical, political, and enforcement factors this model does not capture.
  • Environmental outcomes (microplastic reduction, ecosystem impact) require empirical field measurement and are deliberately not estimated here — no conversion factor for those effects has been supplied.
  • Results are scenario projections under stated assumptions — "what could happen if" — not guaranteed outcomes or a certified statistical forecast.
  • The reporting/verification funnel mixes expected-value stage counts (reports, duplicates, evidence, matching) with individual per-agent violation draws for the persistent accountability history; this is a modelling simplification, not a fully agent-level event log of every report.
